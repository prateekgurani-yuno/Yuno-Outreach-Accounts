## Notes
- Indian astrology app connecting users with astrologers for chat and call consultations; wallet top-ups charged per minute.
- ⛔ **CORRECTION 2026-10-03 — the "Orchestrator on file: Juspay" line was WRONG and has been removed.**
  It said *"If confirmed, this is a displacement motion, not greenfield. Do not open with 'you have
  no orchestration'."* **That warning was backwards.** Three reasons, the first verified by me:
  1. **Razorpay published a dedicated InstaAstro case study** (3 Feb 2025) naming Cards SR Boosters,
     UPI for wallet recharges and its International Payment gateway. I fetched it: 18,480 b,
     **20 hits for "instaastro", ZERO hits for "juspay"**. No orchestrator, router or second PSP is
     named anywhere on it. — https://razorpay.com/blog/razorpay-supercharged-instaastros/
  2. A fetched Juspay customer list carried no astrology, spiritual or consultation app at all.
  3. **Timing kills it:** Razorpay and Cashfree both suspended integrations via third-party
     orchestrators in **January 2025** — the same month that case study was published. A
     Juspay-fronting-Razorpay setup would have broken in exactly that window.
  Strictly this is absence of evidence, not proof InstaAstro never touched Juspay — but the line
  does not survive, and acting on it would have produced the wrong opening.
- ✅ **Actual motion: GREENFIELD. Single gateway, Razorpay direct.** Verbatim from that page:
  *"By **supporting over 80% of InstaAstro's international transactions via cards**, Razorpay is
  helping InstaAstro cater to users worldwide."*
  ⚠️ Note that is a **share of volume**, not a success rate — do not confuse it with the separate
  *"consistent payment success rate of over 80%"* line on Razorpay's **Astrotalk** page. Different
  metrics that happen to share a number.
- **The opening observation:** single-provider concentration on cross-border cards. Razorpay's own
  page says InstaAstro faced *"payment failures were a major hurdle"* across **North America,
  Europe, SEA and MENA**.
- **Scale:** FY26 revenue **₹111.26 Cr** (FY25 ₹52 Cr); claims ₹200 Cr+ annualised; **$12M Series A**
  led by Singularity AMC with Artha Venture Fund; 12M registered users, 5,000+ experts, 183
  countries, **~25% of revenue international**. All `[UNVERIFIED — search summary only]`.
