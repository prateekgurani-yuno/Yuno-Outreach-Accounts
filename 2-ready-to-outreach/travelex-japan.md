### Corrections made during this run
1. ⛔ **"90+ stores in Japan" is WRONG.** The figure is **60+** (Jan 2025), down from ~79 (2017). The estate is **contracting**, not growing.
2. ⛔ **Finablr is NOT the parent.** A search summary asserted it; Finablr collapsed in 2020. Ownership is Barings (>50%) plus Vector, Corre and Mariner.
3. ⛔ **The ¥50bn "Japan revenue" figure cannot be revenue.** Audited **Asia revenue in total is £80.2m ≈ ¥15bn**. ¥50bn for Japan alone is arithmetically impossible as revenue — it is almost certainly **取扱高 (gross exchange turnover)**. Source was a recruitment page.
4. ⛔ **The FY2025 figures that looked irreconcilable are correct.** £477.2m described as "6.6% behind 2024" only reconciles against the **audited £511.1m** FY2024 base in the segment note — not against the £465.0m press figure, which is a different scope. Resolved by reading the statements.
5. ⛔ **The restructuring split is narrower than reported to me.** Not "South America, Middle East, Africa and Asia" but specifically **Brazil, Middle East & Turkey, Nigeria and Asia Pacific**. ⚠️ And note the 21 Apr 2026 group list still contains the UK, Netherlands, Germany, Switzerland, Czech and Canada entities — so **"warehouse Travelex" businesses appear to remain in the group.** Do not claim Asia is the only continuing business.
6. ⛔ **I nearly reported that Japan's checkout runs on a UK domain.** `checkout.travelex.co.uk` does appear on travelex.co.jp — but the links are `/gb/purchase`, sitting in a shared global template. **The Japanese purchase flow is `buy.travelex.co.jp`.** The real finding is adjacent: **different checkout domains per market.**
