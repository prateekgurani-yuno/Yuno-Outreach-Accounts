Wego is a Singapore-registered, Dubai-operated travel platform that has crossed from metasearch into being a real merchant: it sells, tickets and services flight and hotel bookings under "Book on Wego", takes payment in 68 currency denominations with a USD FX base, charges its own booking and processing fees, and runs a Payments & Operations department under a Director of Payments, Risk & Fintech. The central payment finding is that **it already bought orchestration — and bought it for two countries.** Juspay's own customer-story page states the deployment as *5 PSPs · 150+ Currencies · 2 Countries* (Saudi Arabia and the UAE), and the September 2026 expansion announcement extends it only to *"additional Middle Eastern markets."* Verified against Wego's own live public payment API, that boundary is visible rail by rail: Saudi gets MADA, SADAD, STC Pay, Tabby and Tamara; Kuwait gets KNET, deema and Taly; Egypt gets Fawry, valu and InstaPay — while the Philippines, Thailand, Vietnam, Japan, Korea and Australia all receive the **same generic card-and-PayPal list with not one local rail**, Indonesia is served by 2010s-era ATM and internet-banking rails with **no QRIS**, and Malaysia has **no FPX or DuitNow**. Meanwhile Pakistan went live with Book on Wego in January 2025 on a fresh travel-agency licence and IATA accreditation (the cause of its ↑199% traffic), and three `Senior Product Manager - Flights OTA` roles are open **in Bangalore, Cairo and Jakarta/Kuala Lumpur right now**. The motion is therefore **displacement/expansion, never greenfield**: the argument is reach beyond the home region, and the incumbent must never be named.

### 3C. What Wego built itself — and this is the part that matters
Separate from the orchestrator, Wego runs its **own booking and payment API surface**, recovered in full from its endpoint constant table:

```
/metasearch/bookings/flights/passenger-info          (+ /agent  ← B2B agent channel)
/metasearch/bookings/flights/v2/payments/options
/metasearch/bookings/flights/payments                (POST — create payment)
/metasearch/bookings/flights/payments/{id}           (poll)
/metasearch/bookings/flights/payments/resolve
/metasearch/bookings/flights/payments/order-data/{id}
/metasearch/bookings/flights/payments/amounts
/metasearch/bookings/flights/addons/insurance | /addons/confirm
/metasearch/bookings/flights/promocodes/reserve | /release
/metasearch/bookings/flights/refund-status
/metasearch/bookings/flights/v2/fare/revalidate
/flights/payment_methods                             (public, per site_code)
```

**Its own internal payment data model, from `HotelBooking`:**
```
paymentChannel · paymentMethodCode · paymentMethodName
paymentFlow      ∈ { CARD, HOSTED_PAYMENT_PAGE, PAYLATER }
supportedCurrencies · defaultCurrency · fxCurrencies
paymentCard: { bin }        ← BIN lookup
suggestedCurrency           ← BIN-driven charge-currency suggestion
channel → paymentMethods[]  ← methods grouped under channels
```

**Its own auth lifecycle, from `FlightBooking` / `PaymentVerification`:**
`AuthPending` → `AuthPendingConfirmation` → `Authorized` / `Captured` / `AuthDeclined` / `FraudDeclined`, with `allowRetry`, `declinedReasonCode`, `communicationMessage`, `redirectLink`, `processingFee` and a `retry` flag on the payment POST. `PaymentVerification` polls every 10 s for up to 20 minutes.

**Read this correctly.** Wego has a **merchant-side payment abstraction of its own** — channels, methods, flows, currencies, BIN logic, auth/capture states — sitting *above* whatever the orchestrator does. That is why the pitch is neither "you need orchestration" (they have it) nor "you have nothing" (they built plenty). **It is: you are maintaining two layers, one of which only covers two countries, while your merchant footprint moves into Asia.**
