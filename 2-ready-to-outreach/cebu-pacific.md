### ⚠️ The one conditional worth re-opening — and the test is five minutes

The only axis that could clear the bar is **international-market local methods.**

Cebu Pacific carried **6.9m international passengers in 2025, growing 14%**, and is adding China, Japan and Vietnam capacity in Q4 2026. Yet its own payment-methods page lists **no Alipay, no WeChat Pay, no UnionPay, no konbini or Pay-easy, and no Korean rails** — and the three wallets it does offer are **PHP-only by Cebu Pacific's own statement**. **Alipay was accepted in 2017 and is absent from the current page** (SunStar, 2 Apr 2017, which also documents BancNet online banking and OTC cash across 1,700+ 7-Eleven and 1,800+ Cebuana Lhuillier outlets — all now gone).

**Why I am not calling that a gap:** nobody has observed the live booking flow from a foreign IP. `www.cebupacificair.com` returns **410 Gone**, `book.cebupacificair.com` does not resolve, the payment-options page has **no Wayback snapshot**, and the help-centre search is bot-blocked. **Geo-conditional method rendering is exactly what an orchestrator does** — a Shanghai IP may well be served Alipay. Judging the international flow from a Philippine-storefront help article would be the precise unverified assumption this repo refuses to make.

> **RE-OPEN TRIGGER:** load the Cebu Pacific checkout **from a CN, JP or KR IP** and confirm whether local methods render. **Zero local methods on a China-origin booking would be a concrete, sourced gap** and would justify a full sequence on international coverage. Anyone with a VPN can settle it.
