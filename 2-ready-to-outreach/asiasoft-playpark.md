### Friction — verified, and the one piece that is not

**CONFIRMED by me on https://playmallguide.playpark.com/th-th/ and /en-sg/:**
- **THB 15 per-transaction fee** on top-ups under THB 350 via online banking, K PLUS and several other channels — a low-ticket economics problem on a wallet whose minimum load is THB 50.
- Wallet points **expire after 365 days**; transaction history retained **90 days** only.
- **Currency is IP-locked**, changeable only every 60 days and only when the balance is under USD 1 — a rigid, hand-rolled multi-currency design.
