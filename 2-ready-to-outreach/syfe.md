> **Insight #1: There is exactly one card flow on this account, and nobody knows who runs it**
> **Evidence:** S4 + S3A — **Selfwealth Premium, A$29/month or A$240/year, auto-renewing, billed to a stored credit card with a A$1.00 verification pre-authorisation** ([Premium](https://help.selfwealth.com.au/hc/en-au/articles/360041510832-What-is-Selfwealth-Premium-and-how-do-I-enable-it), [pre-auth](https://help.selfwealth.com.au/hc/en-au/articles/360051682591-What-is-the-1-00-charge-on-my-credit-card)) — against S3A's finding that **no card PSP is identifiable** on any of four public assets, and S6's note that Selfwealth has **130,000+ Australian investors** ([CFOtech](https://cfotech.com.au/story/selfwealth-by-syfe-relaunches-app-with-auto-invest-tool)).
> **Pain Point:** A recurring card subscription on an optional product is the most churn-sensitive revenue a platform can own. Every expired, reissued or soft-declined card is a cancelled membership — and Selfwealth's own documentation gives no evidence of retry logic, account updater, network tokens or acquirer failover on that flow, where its competitor documents a seven-day dunning window in writing (S11).
> **Yuno Value Proposition:** Orchestration of the stored-card lifecycle — network tokens so reissued cards keep billing, intelligent retries timed to issuer behaviour, and a second acquirer so one processor's outage is not a month of lost memberships. This is the one place on this account where Yuno's product touches the money.
> **Best Success Case:** A **subscription-billing** reference — authorisation-rate and involuntary-churn improvement on stored cards. **Not** a cross-border acquiring case; that would be the wrong story here. (Reference to be selected from the publicly-referenceable set at write-time; no numbers attached.)
> **Outreach Angle:** *"Selfwealth Premium renews on a stored card every month, with a A$1 pre-auth to check the card is still good. Who handles the retries when it isn't?"*
> **Suggested Subject Line:** `the A$1 charge on Selfwealth Premium`
