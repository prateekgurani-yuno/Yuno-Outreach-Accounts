## Executive Summary

StarHub is a Singapore-listed telco (S$2.4bn FY2025 revenue, 2.2M mobile subscribers), selling only in Singapore under four consumer brands. The one payment stack that is visible publicly belongs to **eight**, its value MVNO, which runs on **Stripe** with PayNow carrying 15% of payments and "hundreds of thousands" of monthly transactions. No orchestrator was detected (greenfield), but the realistic opening is **multi-brand consolidation and cost-of-acceptance under a 20–25% EBITDA squeeze**, not cross-border reach or APM breadth.

### Section 10: Strategic Insights & Outreach Angles

> **Insight #1: Four brands, at least two stacks, no layer across them**
> **Evidence:** S3A — eight runs on Stripe as its "common payments backbone" for eight only ([Stripe](https://stripe.com/customers/starhub-eight)); flagship StarHub uses separate card/GIRO/biller channels ([StarHub support](https://www.starhub.com/business/support/article.html?id=gqCrOiZlHX9o6FGGGIiMv9)). S6 — MyRepublic fully acquired Aug 2025; SGX deck lists four consumer brands (StarHub, eight, giga!, MyRepublic).
> **Pain Point:** `[INFERENCE, not confirmed]` separate gateway contracts, separate fraud rules, separate reconciliation per brand; no volume leverage across ~2.2M mobile + 568K broadband subscribers when negotiating MDR.
> **Yuno Value Proposition:** one integration across brands, consolidated volume for acquirer negotiation, shared routing and fraud rules while each brand keeps its own checkout.
> **Best Success Case:** No telco reference with a sourced multi-brand story found — ask internally.
> **Outreach Angle:** eight was allowed to build its own stack to move fast. That was the right call in 2024. With MyRepublic in the fold and EBITDA guided down 20–25%, the question is who owns cost-of-acceptance across all four brands.
> **Suggested Subject Line:** eight, giga!, MyRepublic — one payments bill?

> **Insight #2: PayNow carries 15% of payments at eight, but it's not in StarHub Prepaid's online top-up list**
> **Evidence:** S4 — StarHub Prepaid app/web: Visa, Mastercard, UnionPay (+ StarHub Dollars in app); PayNow not listed ([StarHub, 28 Apr 2026](https://www.starhub.com/personal/how-to/how-to-top-up-prepaid-plan.html)). S3A — eight: 15% of payments via PayNow on Stripe ([Stripe](https://stripe.com/customers/starhub-eight)). S1 — Singapore 82.21%, 88.78% mobile web.
> **Pain Point:** prepaid users without a credit card (eight's own case study names foreign domestic workers, migrant workers, students, seniors as the value segment) are pushed to AXS or 7-Eleven cash rather than a two-tap online rail. `[INFERENCE, not confirmed]` some share of top-ups leaks offline or lapses.
> **Yuno Value Proposition:** add PayNow (and wallets) to every brand's flow through one integration rather than per-brand builds.
> **Best Success Case:** No sourced match — use eight's own 15% as the internal benchmark.
> **Outreach Angle:** Your own MVNO runs 15% of payments through PayNow. The StarHub Prepaid top-up page lists cards only. Was that a choice?
> **Suggested Subject Line:** 15% at eight, 0% listed at StarHub Prepaid

> **Insight #3: Margin squeeze makes cost-of-acceptance a CFO topic now**
> **Evidence:** S6 — FY2026 EBITDA guided at 75–80% of FY2025; "sharpening cost discipline" ([StarHub](https://corporate.starhub.com/about-us/newsroom/2026/february/starhub-reports-fy2025-results.html)). S12 — 2,205K mobile subs at $22 blended ARPU, 568K broadband subs ([SGX deck](https://links.sgx.com/1.0.0/corporate-announcements/6WSP5L91T8493ZIH/874826_FY2025PS.pdf)).
> **Pain Point:** consumer ARPU flat at $22 and mobile service revenue down 7.7% in FY2025 — every basis point of MDR on monthly bills is margin.
> **Yuno Value Proposition:** acquirer competition and least-cost routing across cards; shifting card volume toward PayNow where the customer prefers it `[INFERENCE — PayNow pricing vs card MDR not sourced]`.
> **Best Success Case:** Yuno telecom ebook (unnamed LATAM telco, failed renewals) — framing only.
> **Outreach Angle:** Guidance says EBITDA drops 20–25% this year on consumer pricing. Payments is one of the few cost lines that falls without touching price or network.
> **Suggested Subject Line:** The cost line that isn't the network

> **Insight #4: Postpaid recurring card billing runs on forms and two-strikes cancellation**
> **Evidence:** S5/S8 — recurring card set up by form; expiry change needs form resubmission; arrangement cancelled after two consecutive same-reason rejections ([StarHub support](https://www.starhub.com/business/support/article.html?id=gqCrOiZlHX9o6FGGGIiMv9)). > **Pain Point:** `[INFERENCE, not confirmed]` expired-card failures and manual re-enrolment drive involuntary churn and collections cost on the flagship postpaid base; the business support article may not reflect the consumer app flow.
> **Yuno Value Proposition:** network tokenization, account updater and smart retries across all brands — what eight already has, extended to the base that carries most revenue.
> **Best Success Case:** Yuno telecom ebook — failed renewals recovered (vendor-reported).
> **Outreach Angle:** eight gets network tokens and account updater from day one. StarHub's postpaid page still asks customers to resubmit a form when their card expires.
> **Suggested Subject Line:** When a StarHub customer's card expires
