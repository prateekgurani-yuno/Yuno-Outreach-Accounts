## What to put here

5–10 of your own best-performing outreach emails. Prioritize, in order:

1. **Emails that got replies** — especially from APAC enterprise prospects.
2. **Emails you'd be happy to see quoted back to you.**
3. **A couple that failed**, marked as such. Knowing what your misses look like is useful.

Paste them raw. Keep your typos and asides — those are the signal. Strip real contact names
and anything confidential; replace the recipient's name with `{{recipient.first_name}}`.

## Format

```
=== SAMPLE 1 · [replied / no reply] · [vertical, market] ===

Subject: ...

Hi {{recipient.first_name}},
...
```

## How the skill uses this

- **Tone only**, not structure. Sentence rhythm, connectives, how you open and close, what
  asides you naturally use, how formal you are.
- The 12-touch structure comes from `full-outreach.md` and overrides sample structure.
- Phase 1 in the sequence is much shorter and more observational than a typical long-form
  prospecting email. If your samples are long and pitch-dense, the skill still compresses.
