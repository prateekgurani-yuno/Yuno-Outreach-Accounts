## Key research flags (read before outreach)

**1. Two account-list figures are refuted, both of which put these companies in the queue.**
- **Strawberrynet `~$150M est.` is a 2017 number.** Traced to a Taipei Times report of May
  2018 (*"average annual sales of about HK$1.1 billion (US$140.13 million)"*), and the same
  stale figure still sits unmaintained on the parent group's own website beside *"over the
  past 20 years"* copy. Current independent estimate: **~US$12m GMV, declining.**
- **Althea `~$30M est.` and "selling cross-border to Southeast Asian shoppers" are both
  false.** FY2025 filed revenue **~US$1,500**, two employees, and **no Southeast Asian
  storefront can process a payment.**

**2. Althea is commercially dead and nobody announced it.** Verified directly: `my`, `ph` and
`sg.althea.kr` all return **`checkoutDisabled: true`** from Shopify's own config endpoint and
Malaysia's `/checkout` returns **403**. Indonesia, Thailand and Taiwan 301 to the US store;
Vietnam, Japan, HK and Korea do not resolve; **`althea.kr` does not complete a TLS
handshake.** The one live store ships to the US and South Korea only. There is no public
statement — the decay is visible only in infrastructure.

**3. Strawberrynet has the best payment architecture in the batch and the smallest business.**
Its live checkout bundle hard-codes the acquirer into the method name:
`WP_PA:["Visa WP-PA","MC WP-PA","Diners WP","JCB WP","Discov WP"]` against
`AW_VISA:"Visa AW"`, with predicates `isAirwallex` and `isWorldPay`. **Visa and Mastercard
run on two acquirers simultaneously, selected by a string comparison in a React hook.**
Rejected anyway: ~343K visits/month and falling caps plausible orders at 7–15k/month.

**4. Amorepacific must never be pitched on volume, and the file says so at the top.** Their
own 1Q26 deck defines the "Online" channel as *"all major platforms (Naver, Kakao, Coupang,
etc.)"*, plus Amazon, Sephora, Tmall, Qoo10, TikTok Shop, Shopee, Olive Young wholesale,
department-store concessions and travel retail at 17% of domestic. **Almost none of the
KRW 4.25tn top line touches an Amorepacific checkout.** The account is a fragmentation story
— five platforms, four providers, nothing shared — not a volume story.

**5. Sociolla's motion is In-house and the sequence is built for it.** `payments-api.sociolla.com`
is a live distinct microservice, verified in the June 2026 capture. Every touch respects the
build; E3 carries the diplomatic clause *"not because anyone built it badly."* **Do not edit
that out.**

**6. Two things about Sociolla are genuinely unknowable from outside, and both matter.**
**QRIS** — the last readable payment inventory is 2019–2020, before QRIS became near-universal,
so its absence is *unchecked*, not sourced. It appears in E3 as a question and nowhere as a
claim. And **Vietnam** — `vn.sociolla.com` carried Midtrans, BCA KlikPay, Kredivo and Vospay
routes in 2022, all **Indonesian** rails, with MoMo, ZaloPay, VNPay and VietQR not found. If
still true it is the sharpest hook on the account; the evidence is four years old.

**7. Missing traffic data cost Stylevana three ICP rows.** Rail gap, traffic-outside-home and
indirectly the volume band all scored zero for want of a SimilarWeb export. On the evidence a
supplied export would plausibly add +5 and land it at ⭐. **13/29 is a floor set by a missing
input, not a ceiling set by the account.**
