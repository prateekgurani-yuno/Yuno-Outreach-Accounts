## Key research flags (read before outreach)

### ⛔ 1. The Wellnet vendor-ceiling conclusion is RETRACTED — it was my own, and it was wrong
The repo previously concluded that Japanese carriers lack code wallets because **Wellnet** sits under their konbini/bank rails and Wellnet's e-money set stops at Rakuten Edy / Mobile Suica / JCB Premo. That causal claim is **refuted by Peach Aviation**, which is a Wellnet customer *and* carries **five code wallets** (楽天ペイ, PayPay, d払い, Alipay, WeChat Pay) — verified first-hand in a dedicated バーコード決済 section of Peach's own availability matrix.

**Wellnet's ceiling binds that one rail, not the airline.** `ana-all-nippon-airways.md` and `japan-airlines-jal.md` have both been patched with explicit retraction blocks.

**For ANA the replacement framing is *stronger*:** the counter-example sits inside ANA's own holding company — **"your own LCC does this and you don't."** ⚠️ The refutation is *causal only*. It does **not** establish that ANA or JAL evaluated wallets and rejected them. Never pitch it as "you were wrong about your vendor."

### ⛔ 2. Moshtix is not TEG — the stub's central premise was wrong
`accounts/apac-tal.csv` line 174 said *"owned by TEG"* / *"Part of TEG."* **Moshtix has been a Ticketmaster (Live Nation) subsidiary since February 2019** — verified on Moshtix's own B2B site, which names *"Ticketmaster, Live Nation Concerts, and Live Nation Media & Sponsorship"* and serves a logo file called `Moshtix-a-Ticketmaster-Comany-ReverseLogo.png`.

**Outreach withheld deliberately.** No Phase 0 gate fires — Moshtix Pty Ltd is Australian, in territory, not a PSP — but a payments decision at a Live Nation subsidiary is plausibly a global-account call, not an APAC SDR one. **This needs a rules-of-engagement decision from Prateek.** Parked like Viu, not abandoned. The Ticketek comparison in the stub was also wrong: it compares against *a competitor's parent*, not a sibling.

### 🔴 4. Peach — a supplier bankruptcy took card acceptance off the aircraft for five months
Peach's own notice: the in-flight payment system's **application developer filed for bankruptcy**, suspending onboard card acceptance **2025-09-01 → 2026-01-26**, cash-only with **no receipts issuable**. The same developer took down **JTA and Solaseed Air simultaneously**. Then on **2026-05-29** a further 「決済システムの変更」 stripped **HKD, THB and SGD** from Alipay and WeChat Pay — on routes Peach flies. Two dated events in twelve months where a supplier, not Peach, decided what Peach could accept.

### ⚙️ 5. Peatix turned off every wallet in their own Stripe element
Verified in the live production bundle: `wallets:{applePay:'never', googlePay:'never', link:'never'}` — while the help centre **still advertises Apple Pay** and reviewers file 1★ reports of Apple Pay erroring. `STRIPE_JYP` and `STRIPE_SGD` in the payment-method enum show Japan and Singapore moving onto Stripe. **A platform mid-re-platform is the best moment to arrive.** The real hook, though, is the **payout leg**: 1,000+ new organisers monthly across 13 currencies, per-event transfers, **no batching, no netting, no balance, no FX**, ¥210 a time, PayPal splitting disbursements. A treasury problem, not an acceptance problem.

### ⚠️ 6. Oztix needs a human with a browser before outreach
Three of its sharpest claims — the **Amex/Diners refusal**, the **Pay Over Time forfeiture clause**, and the **"payment gateway is experiencing high demand"** FAQ line — **could not be re-reached** and remain agent-sourced only. The Zendesk help-centre-API technique that cracked Ticketek, Moshtix and eplus **fails here — Oztix does not run Zendesk.** No mineable CSP, no un-WAF'd bundle, so **the gateway is unnamed.** What *is* verified: the privacy policy's **singular** "our payment gateway provider" (twice), the MoR entity, the direct-debit rail, and every `/about/` figure.

### 🧰 7. Techniques and false positives
- **The B2B-route technique worked again** on Oztix — `/about/` is plain S3/CloudFront while the consumer estate is a JS SPA.
- **Moshtix's `cdn.moshtix.com.au` is un-WAF'd** and serves the booking bundle **un-minified with comments intact** — the richest single artefact in this batch.
- **Zendesk decoys:** `moshtix.zendesk.com` returns **0 articles**; the live desks are `moshtix-au.zendesk.com` (locale `en-au`, 126 articles) and `clientsupport.moshtix.com` (99 articles). **When the obvious host returns zero, check `/locales.json` and look for a separate B2B desk — the B2B desk is consistently richer.**
- **Two NEW false-positive classes:**
  - **`useContext` → eContext** (11 hits in Peach's bundle; would have produced a clean-looking fabricated vendor name)
  - **A Sentry `ignoreErrors` regex array → PSP hits.** Peatix's bundle carried `/^Can't find variable: gmo$/`, which reads as GMO Payment Gateway and is not.
