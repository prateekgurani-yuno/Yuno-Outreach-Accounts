## Key research flags (read before outreach)

**1. ⛔ Our own skill file carried a false number and it would have reached a prospect.**
`.claude/commands/full-outreach.md` said Cathay Pacific *"expanded to Adyen direct acquiring
across **45+ markets**"*. I fetched the Adyen release: **it is six markets** — Hong Kong,
Australia, New Zealand, the US, Japan and India — and **the number 45 appears nowhere on the
page or in any syndication of it**. Walking into Cathay citing 45 would have been repeating a
number the merchant knows is false. **The skill file is fixed and carries an inline warning.**
This is the second wrong number caught in our own reference material this week; both were found
by fetching the primary source rather than trusting the file.

**2. ⛔ The ANA PayPay hook was set up, gated, and then killed by its own gate.**
ANA's exhaustive first-party method table has **no PayPay** — Japan's dominant wallet, missing
from Japan's largest airline. That looked like the opener. I gated it on checking JAL first, then
checked: **JAL takes no PayPay either, and no Apple Pay, Rakuten Pay, LINE Pay, d払い, au PAY,
Amazon Pay, Google Pay or Paidy.** It is a Japanese legacy-carrier category norm. Leading on it
earns the true and fatal reply *"JAL doesn't either."* **Demoted out of the opener into an E3
roadmap question.** The score was held at 23 with the reasoning stated in the row rather than
quietly split — Prateek may reasonably want that row at +1.

**3. 🟡 Cebu Pacific was stood down, and the pre-set test is what stood it down.**
The competitive-motion rule says proceed only if research surfaces a concrete gap. The test set
beforehand was *"GCash absent = gap; GCash present = stand down."* I checked Cebu's own help
centre: **GCash, GrabPay, Maya and PayPal are all present.** The test resolved against us and I
honoured it. **It stays in `2-ready-to-outreach/` at 🟡 rather than moving to `not-icp/`, because
the blocker is competitive motion, not fit** — CellPoint Digital is incumbent with a published
case study. If a concrete gap ever surfaces, the file is ready to run.
