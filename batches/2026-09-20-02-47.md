## Key research flags (read before outreach)

**The two rejections, and why they are different from each other.**
- **VNGGames — Phase 0, not a scoring decision.** VNG Limited's own SEC Form F-1 (CIK 1930799)
  states it holds *"licenses to operate an electronic payment gateway, to maintain digital wallets
  on behalf of users and to maintain an intermediary payment services permit"* and *"We also
  operate the ZaloPay payment gateway."* That routes to Partnerships, not sales.
- **Devsisters — analyst override on the app-store trap.** Their own help centre: *"we neither
  process in-app purchases, nor have access to your credit card information. All purchases are
  processed by Apple and Google Play."* 94.25% of audited revenue is store-billed. The only
  self-billed channel is ~US$8.2M/yr of merchandise at an estimated 5–16k orders/month — about a
  tenth of the floor. **Matrix score was 17/29; the override, not the score, drove it.** Revisit
  trigger is in the file: goods sales tripled FY24→FY25 and IP revenue passed 10% of total in H1
  2026.

**Com2uS is a DISPLACEMENT sequence, and the stub did not say so.** PortOne holds the orchestration
seat inside the Hive billing platform — verified on Hive's own production documentation, which
instructs merchants to register the IAP callback in the PortOne admin console and imports the PG
list from there. Opening with "you have no orchestration layer" would be factually wrong. Per
`/full-outreach` §6a, open on coverage and reach, and never name the incumbent.

**Webzen is one-fifth the size the target list implies, and that must be said internally before
anyone builds a business case.** The audited FY2025 segment note splits revenue into internet
22.8% (Webzen bills the player), royalty 39.1% (Gameforge and five Chinese licensees bill) and
mobile 37.8% (Apple/Google bill). Addressable is KRW 39.8bn ≈ US$28M, ~18% of the top line. The
"~$150M (FY24)" on file is technically correct and practically misleading — FY2025 came in at
KRW 174.4bn, down 18.8%.

**Three target-list revenue figures were wrong, in both directions.**
- Asphere/PlayPark "~$100M (FY24)" — **refuted, a 3× overstatement.** Audited FY2024 was THB
  1,120.3M (≈US$32M). Also: the company renamed to Asphere Innovations in 2023, and Malaysia
  books THB 0 since the Q3 2023 disposal.
- Gravity "~$300M (FY24)" — **understated ~15%.** FY2025 is US$388.0M audited. Indonesia is the
  #2 market at 15.2%, up 163% YoY, and was missing from the operating-country list entirely.
- Devsisters "~$150M (FY24)" — **understated ~13–20%.** Audited FY2024 is KRW 236.19bn.
- Com2uS "~$500M (FY24)" and Cygames "~$1B est." — both **confirmed**. Cygames turned out not to
  be an estimate at all: CyberAgent discloses an audited entity-level figure of ¥137,528M.

**The app-store trap was the decisive question for all ten, and it resolved differently each time.**
It fired once (Devsisters). It partially applies at Gravity (81.2% mobile, web-vs-IAP split not
disclosed — no email may imply one) and at Com2uS (IAP still dominates; the web shop is three
months old and gated to accounts with 2026+ purchase history). It was cleanly escaped at SEAGM
(the app routes through `pay.seagm.com` in a React Native webview, not IAP), at Cygames (merchant
of record in its own name, registered prepaid-instrument issuer) and at HAGO (store billing is two
channels among ~25 in their own router, plus Huawei/Xiaomi/Aptoide builds with no Play obligation).

**⚠️ Carry this into any JOYY conversation: JOYY owns an app called Yuno.** `com.joyy.yuno`,
`yunoliveapp.com`, `APP_NAME.YUNO`. Grepping their bundle for "yuno" returns 57 hits and none of
them is us. Anyone who skims and reports "Yuno is already integrated" will be wrong in a way that
is very hard to walk back on a call.

**Two accounts have a hook that is a single quoted sentence from the prospect's own paperwork —
these are the two to send first.**
- **Gravity:** the FY2025 20-F discloses payment-processing commission of 1.4–2.8% in Korea against
  5–21% Taiwan, 1.5–24% Thailand and 0.7–30% LatAm. An audited asymmetry inside their own stack.
  Their LatAm help centre separately concedes *"we're currently working to expand our support for
  more currencies very soon."*
- **Asphere:** their FY2024 annual report says *"By having more payment channels, the operational
  cost for the Company is reduced."* They wrote the business case themselves.

**Methodology note worth keeping.** The `form-action` rule held up and paid off: Gravity's Taiwan
and Thailand CSPs both carry `form-action` directives that name four PSPs outright, making those
the only sourced absences in the batch. Every other checkout host in these ten — Cygames, SEAGM,
HAGO, Webzen, Com2uS, Asphere — returns either no CSP at all or one without `form-action`, so no
PSP can be ruled out by header inference on any of them. Each file states which case it is in.

**Pipeline hygiene, flagged by an agent and worth acting on.** One research agent found its
scratchpad JS directory still held bundles from a *different* account earlier in this session, and
its first grep returned a "LINE Pay" hit that was not the prospect's. It caught the error by
printing context before accepting the hit. Recommend scoping scratchpad subdirectories per account
as standing practice — this near-miss would have put a fabricated payment method into an email.
