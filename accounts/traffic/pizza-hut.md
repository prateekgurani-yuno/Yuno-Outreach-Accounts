## Read before using this data

1. **Sales Intelligence view, not Web Intelligence.** Only the top 10 countries per domain are shown, with traffic share only. Per-country visit counts were NOT supplied; the "Est. Monthly Visits" column is intentionally left blank. If you need a per-country figure, derive it as share × that domain's avg monthly visits and label it `[DERIVED from supplied share, not a SimilarWeb figure]`.
2. **Shares are single-domain.** Each table is that domain's own split. To combine domains, convert to visits per domain first (share × domain monthly visits), then sum and recalculate. Never average percentages across domains.
3. **Mobile app traffic is not included.** Similarweb counts desktop and mobile-web visits only.
4. **App-heavy business: web traffic understates transactions.** Most orders happen in the mobile app (Pizza Hut apps per market) and via aggregators (GrabFood, foodpanda, Swiggy, Zomato); this is QSR food delivery. Similarweb web data does not capture app traffic. Do NOT size payment volume, transaction count or the 40k/month Phase-0 gate from web traffic alone. Use the country split as a market-mix signal only, and look for app downloads, MAU, GMV or order figures from a sourced filing or press release before sizing.
5. Pizza Hut is franchised by market (different operators and different PSPs). Domains pulled: pizzahut.com, pizzahut.co.in, pizzahut.com.my. Note: `accounts/apac-tal.csv` has a separate "Pizza Hut Malaysia" row.
