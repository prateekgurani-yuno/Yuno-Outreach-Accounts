## Analyst notes
- **Home market is 55.26% — under the 60% threshold.** ICP signal "high traffic outside
  home market" is MET on this data.
- **Diaspora pattern confirmed and it is the whole story.** Roughly 45% of traffic sits
  outside India, spread across US, UAE, UK, Australia, Saudi, Canada, Germany, Netherlands,
  Malaysia. Nine distinct issuing geographies in the top twelve.
- **US engagement is the outlier**: 8:22 average visit against India's 2:31, and 5.30 pages
  per visit. The US audience is smaller but far more engaged — a paying-subscriber
  signature, not a browsing one.
- Traffic is declining in most markets (India −34%, US −45%, Bangladesh −57%). UK is the
  exception at +45%. Note it; do not build an outreach hook on decline — it reads as an
  insult in a cold email.
- UAE and Saudi are EMEA territory markets, but YuppTV is India-HQ so the account is in APAC
  scope. Cross-border framing on those corridors still applies.
