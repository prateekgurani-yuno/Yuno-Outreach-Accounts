## What this traffic does and does not support

**Scores 0 on "high traffic outside home market."** The ICP row requires the home market to
sit **below 60%** of traffic. Singapore is **84.68%** — more than double the threshold. This
is a domestically-concentrated property, and the row must be scored **0**, not flexed.

**The Philippines row is the only live expansion signal in the dataset.** 9.06% share at
**▲244.96% MoM** is a step-change, not drift. Two readings, and the traffic alone cannot
separate them:
- a genuine market entry or local campaign, or
- a single referral/affiliate spike, or bot traffic.

The engagement metrics argue for caution on the second reading: a **26-second** average
session against a **77.92%** bounce rate is service-and-leave behaviour, not shopping
behaviour. For an insurer this is unremarkable — policyholders log in, check a balance or a
claim, and leave — but it means **visit volume is a poor proxy for transaction volume here,**
and no transaction count should be derived from it.

**The 3-domain scope is unresolved.** The pull spans `singlife.com` plus two further domains
which the supplied view did not name. Until they are identified, the country shares are an
**aggregate across an unknown estate** — if one of the two is a Philippines property, the
9.06% may be concentrated there rather than spread across the group. Flagged, not assumed.
