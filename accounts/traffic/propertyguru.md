## ⚠️ Analyst notes

**1. This is a TWO-MARKET business. Singapore + Malaysia = 88.87%.** Everything else is a
rounding error — third place is 1.57%. Any rail analysis beyond **PayNow/cards (SG)** and
**FPX/DuitNow/cards (MY)** is wasted effort. ⚠️ Singapore at 47.03% is below 60%, so the
*"high traffic outside home"* row technically fires — but **flag the double-count risk**: it
fires only because Malaysia is enormous, not because the business is globally distributed.

**2. ⭐ Malaysia has MORE distinct users than Singapore.** Malaysia's **audience share 47.94%**
exceeds its traffic share 41.84%, while Singapore's audience share (37.12%) is *below* its
traffic share (47.03%). So Singapore users visit more often; **Malaysia has the larger user
base.** If monetisation is per-user or per-listing, Malaysia may matter more than the headline
traffic order suggests.

**3. ✅ Engagement is very high and clearly real** — 6.6–16.3 pages/visit across the board,
bounce as low as 16.96% (Japan) and 19.54% (Hong Kong). Classic classifieds browsing. No junk
rows in this table.

**4. Both core markets are GROWING** (SG ↑5.68%, MY ↑6.10%) — unusual in this pipeline, where
most accounts are contracting. Indonesia ↓43.58% is the one sharp decline and sits at 0.82%.

**5. 🛑 THE BILLING-UNIT QUESTION THAT DECIDES THIS ACCOUNT.** PropertyGuru is a **classifieds
marketplace**: consumers browse for free, and revenue comes from **agents and developers paying
for listings, subscriptions, credits and ad products** — a **B2B recurring/credit model**, not a
consumer checkout. Establish this before any pitch is written, exactly as with a wealth manager:
*who actually pays, how often, by what rail, and in what currency.* Agent subscriptions billed
by card or direct debit in two markets is a very different (and smaller) orchestration
conversation than a consumer marketplace. Also check for any **consumer-side** payment
(mortgage, booking fees, FinTech/home-loan products) which would change the picture.

**6. ⚠️ 4 unnamed additional domains** — almost certainly `propertyguru.com.my`,
`propertyguru.com.sg` plus Thai/Indonesian properties (DDproperty, Rumah123 and Batdongsan are
group brands). **Resolve them, check redirects, and note that group brands may be separate
properties with separate payment stacks.**
