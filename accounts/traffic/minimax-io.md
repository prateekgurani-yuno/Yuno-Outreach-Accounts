## ⚠️ Domain scope — read this before using the data

**Verified by me on 2026-09-24 by following redirects:**

| Domain | Resolves to | In this dataset? |
|---|---|---|
| `minimax.io` | → `www.minimax.io` (200) | ✅ yes |

| `agent.minimax.io` | 200, no redirect | ✅ yes |
| **`minimaxi.com`** | **→ `minimax.cn`** | ❌ **no — this is the China property** |
| **`hailuo.ai`** | **→ `agent.minimax.cn`** (4 redirects) | ❌ **no — China** |
| **`hailuoai.com`** | 200, standalone | ❌ **no — separate domain** |
| **`hailuoai.video`** | 200, standalone | ❌ **no — separate domain** |
| **`talkie-ai.com`** | 200, standalone | ❌ **no — separate domain** |

**Two consequences, and both matter.**

1. ⭐ **`minimax.io` is MiniMax's international property.** The Chinese business lives on `minimax.cn` / `agent.minimax.cn` and is **not** in this dataset. That is why China shows only 3.24%. Read this data as *MiniMax's outbound international business*, not as the company.
2. ⚠️ **The consumer apps are invisible here.** Hailuo (AI video) and Talkie (AI companion) sit on `hailuoai.com`, `hailuoai.video` and `talkie-ai.com` — **separate domains, none in this export.** Those are the most likely homes of high-count consumer subscription volume. **This dataset therefore covers the developer/API/agent business only, and probably understates MiniMax's total addressable transaction count.** Ask Prateek for `hailuoai.com` and `talkie-ai.com` traffic before sizing the account.
