## Read

**This is a US-led, globally-diffuse property, not an APAC one.** The United States alone is
**27.44%** — more than four times the next market. **Visible APAC territory totals ~14.3%**
(Indonesia 6.13 + Bangladesh 2.32 + Australia 2.20 + India 2.18 + Korea 1.47), spread across
five markets of which none exceeds 6.2%.

⚠️ **Kazakhstan (3.35%) is deliberately marked ambiguous.** CLAUDE.md defines the territory as
"everything east of Dubai" but its market table lists only South Asia, SEA, Greater China,
North Asia and Oceania. Central Asia is not enumerated. **Not counted in the APAC total above**
— flag for Prateek rather than resolved unilaterally.

🇦🇪 **UAE (1.52%) is explicitly out of territory** — Gulf sits with EMEA.

### ⚠️ The Indonesia row should not be used as an APAC case

Indonesia is the #2 market at **6.13%** and **▲484.29% MoM**, which looks like the strongest
APAC signal in the dataset. The engagement metrics say otherwise:

| | 🇮🇩 Indonesia | 🇺🇸 United States |
|---|---|---|
| Visit duration | **00:00:13** | 00:04:12 |
| Pages / visit | 2.92 | **9.02** |
| Bounce | 56.59% | 22.21% |

**Thirteen seconds.** A near-500% MoM jump landing on 13-second sessions is the profile of
referral, incentivised or non-human traffic, not of users completing a crypto swap — a flow
that takes minutes and multiple pages, exactly as the US rows show. **Treat the Indonesia
spike as unverified volume. Do not build an APAC volume argument on it.**

🇲🇽 **Mexico is the engagement outlier worth noting** — 00:19:05 average session at 8.82
pages. Whatever real depth-of-use exists in this dataset is in the Americas, not APAC.
