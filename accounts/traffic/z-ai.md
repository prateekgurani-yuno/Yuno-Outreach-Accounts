## Analyst notes

- **There is no home market.** China is **third at 8.33%**, behind India (11.20%) and the US (8.78%).
  No country exceeds 11.2%, across **122 countries**. This is the flattest traffic distribution of any
  account in this pipeline — and it means the ICP "high traffic outside home" row scores **+2** on the
  widest possible margin.
- ⚠️ **The reason China is low is structural, not weak demand.** `z.ai` is Zhipu's *international*
  property (it redirects to `chat.z.ai`); Chinese users go to `bigmodel.cn` / `chatglm.cn`, which are
  **not in this dataset**. **z.ai traffic ≈ Zhipu's outbound international business**, and should be read
  that way throughout.
- **APAC in-territory ≈ 39.3%** — India 11.20, China 8.33, Indonesia 5.57, Vietnam 4.44, Bangladesh 3.87,
  Pakistan 3.03, Thailand 1.66, Korea 1.23.
- **Out of territory:** US 8.78, Russia 6.87, Egypt 5.04 (EMEA), Brazil 2.60, Canada 2.53, Germany 2.10,
  UK 1.54, France 1.51, Italy 1.33, Mexico 1.29, Spain 1.20.
- **Almost everything is declining.** Only **four** of the top 19 grew: China ▲38.24%, Russia ▲3.76%,
  Egypt ▲3.53%, Italy ▲3.30%. Vietnam fell **55.49%**, Korea 41.26%, Mexico 38.87%, Pakistan 38.57%,
  Indonesia 32.55%. ⚠️ **A broad decline across a young AI product usually means competitive substitution
  or a change in free-tier limits — establish which before any outreach leans on growth.**
- ⚠️ **Canada is an outlier and should not be read as engagement:** 45:26 average visit and 11.84
  pages/visit against a 2.53% share. Long-session automated or developer traffic is the likelier
  explanation. Do not build anything on it.
- ⚠️ **No absolute visit count was supplied**, so monthly transactions cannot be derived from traffic.
