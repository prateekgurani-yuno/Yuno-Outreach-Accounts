## Analyst notes

- ⭐ **India is the #1 market at 18.93%, and it is a real audience, not SEO noise.** This is the single most important line in the dataset. India carries the **lowest bounce rate in the table at 22.42%**, **5.89 pages per visit**, and **02:25** average duration. Its **audience share (21.97%) exceeds its traffic share (18.93%)**, meaning proportionally more unique users than visits.
- ⭐ **India is 46% larger than the United States** (18.93% vs 13.00%). InVideo is not a US-first product with incidental Indian traffic.
- ⭐ **Emerging markets dominate in aggregate.** India 18.93 + Brazil 6.91 + Pakistan 2.64 + Philippines 2.21 + Indonesia 2.03 + Venezuela 1.77 = **34.49% from the visible nine alone**, before the 113-country tail. Against a checkout that accepts **Credit Card, Apple Pay and Google Pay only, priced in USD only** — see `2-ready-to-outreach/invideo-ai.md`.
- **Engagement is strong across the board**, unlike some accounts in this pipeline: every visible market runs 01:42 or longer with 4.47+ pages per visit. There is no bounce-level filler row here except arguably the Philippines.
- ⚠️ **The Philippines has the highest bounce in the table at 61.36%** despite 4.47 pages/visit and 02:00 duration. Odd combination; worth a second look before leaning on that market.
- ⚠️ **Venezuela is an outlier and should not be built on.** 1.77% share, but **14.86 pages/visit** (2.5× the next highest), **06:18** duration, and **down 80.22%**. That profile reads as automated or scripted traffic rather than users, and the collapse suggests something changed. Exclude from any argument.
