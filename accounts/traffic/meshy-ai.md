## What this traffic does and does not support

**Scores on "high traffic outside home market" — but the home market has to be established
first.** The top market takes only **11.95%**, so no single country dominates and the
long tail across **119 countries** is the whole business. Whether this row fires depends
entirely on where Meshy is actually HQ'd and billing from, which is the open question in
this run. If the billing entity is APAC, a US top market at 11.95% fires the row
comfortably. If the billing entity is US, the row is a different calculation and the
**territory premise itself** needs re-examining.

**The growth direction is the outlier in this cohort, and it is the most useful signal here.**
Every other AI account in this batch is contracting — Kimi declining across all 13 recorded
markets, PictureThis declining across its entire top 9. Meshy is **growing in most markets**.
A broadening 119-country footprint on an expanding base is exactly the profile where
per-market payment coverage starts to bind: the markets being added are, by definition, the
ones the existing stack was not built for.

**No transaction count can be derived from this file.** No total-visit figure was shown, and
no conversion rate is known. Any volume figure must come from pricing and
subscriber disclosures, not from traffic — and must be tagged SOURCED or DERIVED accordingly.
