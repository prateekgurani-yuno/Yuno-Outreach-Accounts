## Analyst notes

- **This is a single-market business by traffic.** Philippines is **86.99%**, and the next market is 1.30%. The top 7 countries together account for 92.33%.
- ⚠️ **The non-Philippine traffic is almost certainly not a customer base.** Look at engagement rather than share: **US 00:00:22 and 1.93 pages**, **Australia 00:00:16 and 1.58 pages**, **Canada 00:00:26**, **Nigeria 00:00:28**. Those are bounce-level sessions. Against the Philippines at **00:02:43 and 3.87 pages**, the difference is categorical, not marginal. **India at 01:18 / 1.72 pages with a 53.83% bounce is the weakest-quality row in the table.** Treat these markets as informational or SEO traffic, not as users, unless something else proves otherwise.
- ⚠️ **This matters for ICP scoring.** Three countries technically exceed 1% share (PH, IN, US), which would mechanically satisfy a "3+ countries" signal. **Awarding it on 22-second US sessions would be gaming the matrix.** See the scoring note in the research file.
- 📉 **The Philippines is down 25.93%** — the home market, and by far the largest decline in absolute terms. Establish the cause before any outreach leans on growth.
- ⚠️ **Nigeria fell 91.02% and shows no country rank at all.** A drop that size with a missing rank reads as a measurement artefact or a collapse to near-zero base rather than organic churn. Do not build on it.
- 📈 Growing, all off small bases: Russia ▲47.59%, Australia ▲17.65%, India ▲5.82%, US ▲4.66%.
- ⚠️ **No absolute visit count was supplied**, so monthly transactions cannot be derived from traffic.
- ⚠️ **Web traffic understates this business.** Coins.ph is app-first — its own positioning is *"The All-in-One E-Wallet & Crypto Super App for Filipinos"*. Web share is not a proxy for transaction volume here, and the country split should not be read as a revenue split.
