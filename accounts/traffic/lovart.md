## ⚠️ Analyst notes

**1. ⭐⭐ CHINA IS THE #1 MARKET AT 19.68%, AND THAT IS THE DEFINING PAYMENT FACT.**
Per `apac-payments.md`, China requires **Alipay, WeChat Pay and UnionPay**, and reaching them
needs a **licensed local path** — it is the hardest acquiring market in the territory. A product
whose largest market is China either has a China-domestic entity and licensed rails, or it is
monetising China badly or not at all. **Establish which, with sources, before anything else.**
This single question is worth more than the rest of the country table combined.

**2. Extremely fragmented and genuinely global: 118 countries**, top market under 20%, and the
listed 11 rows total only **68.39%** — nearly a third of traffic sits outside them. The
*"high traffic outside home"* row fires decisively regardless of where HQ turns out to be.

**3. ✅ Engagement is strong and real.** 3–12 minute sessions, 2.3–14.5 pages/visit, bounce
20–48%. **Japan is exceptional at 00:12:10 and 14.48 pages/visit**; India 00:08:41 / 8.59 and
Morocco 00:06:17 / 8.26 are also deep. ⭐ **India's audience share (9.11%) far exceeds its
traffic share (5.66%)** — many distinct users. Treat all eleven as real markets.

**4. 🚩 Eight of eleven markets are declining**, several steeply (US ↓26.69%, Pakistan ↓32.61%,
Morocco ↓25.97%, HK ↓19.24%). Only Brazil and India are up. **Do not write an expansion
narrative.** As with other AI accounts in this pipeline, this looks like a cooling hype cycle.

**5. ⚠️ IDENTITY MUST BE SETTLED FIRST.** Prateek's argument read "Lovart (liblib AI)", but
**Lovart and LiblibAI are not obviously the same company** — Lovart is an AI design agent,
LiblibAI a Chinese AI image-generation platform. He has confirmed the target is **`lovart.ai`,
matching this data**. The research must still **establish the corporate relationship, the real
HQ and the billing entity**, because those decide territory, the acquiring path and whether any
export-control or sanctions screening question applies (a China-origin AI company in this repo
has already needed that check once).

**6. Rails implied:** China → **Alipay/WeChat Pay/UnionPay, licensed path required** · US →
cards, PayPal, Apple/Google Pay · Brazil → **Pix, boleto** · India → **UPI, cards** · Japan →
**konbini, PayPay** · Taiwan → **JKOPay, ATM transfer** · Egypt → **Fawry** · Pakistan →
**JazzCash, Easypaisa** · Morocco → local cards/cash. **A USD-card-only stack would be missing
the dominant rail in most of its top ten.**
