## What this corrects

signals depended on which you believed. That is now resolved, and **both scrapes were wrong
in different ways**:

| Israel | **#2 at 13.39%** | **#4 at 4.08%** |
| Singapore | not exposed | **#2 at 5.15%** |
| Germany | #4 at 5.42% | #7 at 3.25% |
| Australia | #5 at 3.81% / #3 at 7.22% | **#6 at 3.40%** |
| Countries >1% | unknown, ranks 6–10 paywalled | **19** |

The Semrush figure for Thailand (46.13%) was close. The SimilarWeb *scrape* (27.25%) was far
off, and its Israel figure was overstated by more than three times.

**The ICP consequence:** the "local rail or licensing gap in a top-3 market" signal was
previously justified on **Australia** having no PayTo or BPAY, on the strength of Semrush
putting Australia third. Australia is **sixth**. That justification is withdrawn. The signal
still scores, on a cleaner basis: **Singapore is the #2 market at 5.15% and PayNow is
sourced-absent** from Bangkok Airways' own enumerated payment page.

## What this file does NOT contain

**Absolute visit volume.** This is a geography export: it gives shares, engagement and
country ranks, not total visits. The earlier estimates of ~612K (SimilarWeb scrape) and
~623K (Semrush) remain **unverified estimates**, and multiplying them by these verified
shares would launder an estimate into a number that looks solid. Do not do it. If absolute
volume matters for a business case, pull the Website Performance export separately.
