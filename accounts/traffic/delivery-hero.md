## Read before using this data

1. **Sales Intelligence view, not Web Intelligence.** Only the top 10 countries per domain are shown, with traffic share only. Per-country visit counts were NOT supplied; the "Est. Monthly Visits" column is intentionally left blank. If you need a per-country figure, derive it as share × that domain's avg monthly visits and label it `[DERIVED from supplied share, not a SimilarWeb figure]`.
2. **Shares are single-domain.** Each table is that domain's own split. To combine domains, convert to visits per domain first (share × domain monthly visits), then sum and recalculate. Never average percentages across domains.
3. **Mobile app traffic is not included.** Similarweb counts desktop and mobile-web visits only.
4. **App-heavy business: web traffic understates transactions.** Most food and grocery orders happen in the mobile app (talabat, Baemin, Glovo and foodpanda apps); this is a food-delivery business. Similarweb web data does not capture app traffic. Do NOT size payment volume, transaction count or the 40k/month Phase-0 gate from web traffic alone. Use the country split as a market-mix signal only, and look for app downloads, MAU, GMV or order figures from a sourced filing or press release before sizing.
5. deliveryhero.com (corporate) was not used. Brand domains pulled: talabat.com, baemin.com, glovoapp.com. foodpanda (Delivery Hero APAC brand) country domains are in `accounts/traffic/foodpanda.md`; use both files together for the APAC picture.
