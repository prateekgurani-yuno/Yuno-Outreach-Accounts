## ⚠️ Analyst notes — read before using this table

**1. India is 62.80%, which is ABOVE the 60% threshold.** The ICP signal *"High traffic outside
home (home country < 60%)"* therefore **does NOT fire and scores 0**. Do not round this down.
Outside-India traffic is 37.20%.

**2. India is the only market GROWING at scale.** India ↑19.79% while the US ↓23.50%,
Australia ↓52.31%, Canada ↓37.89% and South Africa ↓71.44%. The three largest Western markets
are all contracting sharply. Any "international expansion is working" framing has to survive
this table.

**3. 🚩 Several international rows look like low-quality or non-human traffic.** Judge the
international footprint on engagement, not share:

**The Audience Share column corroborates this.** Sri Lanka carries 2.68% of traffic but only
**0.77%** of audience share, and UAE 2.38% against 1.91%. Where audience share sits well below
traffic share, the visits are concentrated in few or repeated sessions rather than distinct users.

**Consequence for the report:** the genuinely addressable non-India markets are
**United States, Canada, United Kingdom and Australia** — the diaspora corridor. Sri Lanka, UAE,
Indonesia, Brazil and the Netherlands should NOT be treated as real markets without better
evidence, and must not be used to claim breadth in outreach.

**4. Web traffic is a weak proxy here.** InstaAstro is an app-first consumer business whose
revenue is wallet top-ups inside the app. These are website visits only. Transaction volume must
be derived from revenue and top-up size, not from this table.
