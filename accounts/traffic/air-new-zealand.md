## Analyst notes
- **Home market is 67.68% — above the 60% threshold.** ICP signal "high traffic outside home
  market" is NOT met. Do not award those 2 points.
- **The Asia growth rates are the story, not the shares.** Taiwan +68.67%, Thailand +51.08%,
  Indonesia +1,596.11%. Small bases, but that is what a route build-out looks like in traffic
  before it looks like it in revenue.
- **Indonesia's 14.88 pages per visit against a ~4–6 site average** is a genuine anomaly.
  Either heavy research behaviour on a new route, or a booking flow those users are
  struggling through. Worth checking in the checkout audit — it is either the best hook in
  this account or a data artefact, and the report must not guess which.
- Six APAC markets in the top eleven outside NZ/AU: Japan, Taiwan, Thailand, Singapore,
  Indonesia, India. Whether Air NZ holds local entities or acquires locally in any of them
  is the central research question for this account.
