## Read before using this data

1. **Sales Intelligence view, not Web Intelligence.** Only the top 10 countries per domain are shown, with traffic share only. Per-country visit counts were NOT supplied; the "Est. Monthly Visits" column is intentionally left blank. If you need a per-country figure, derive it as share × that domain's avg monthly visits and label it `[DERIVED from supplied share, not a SimilarWeb figure]`.
2. **Shares are single-domain.** Each table is that domain's own split. To combine domains, convert to visits per domain first (share × domain monthly visits), then sum and recalculate. Never average percentages across domains.
3. **Mobile app traffic is not included.** Similarweb counts desktop and mobile-web visits only.
4. B2B QR payment gateway (StarPay): its payment volume runs through merchant terminals and partner wallets, not netstars.co.jp, so web traffic says nothing about transaction volume. Phase-0 risk: NetStars is a payment gateway. Check the PSP / gateway rule before a full run.
