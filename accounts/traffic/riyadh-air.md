## Read

### The home market sits below 60% — the outside-home ICP row fires

**Saudi Arabia is 52.36%.** The ICP matrix awards +2 where the home country is **under 60%** of
traffic, and this clears it by 7.6 points. **Just under half of all demand originates outside the
home market**, which is unusual for a carrier barely out of launch and is the single most useful
number in this file.

### APAC is the largest non-home bloc, which is why this account is here at all

**Visible APAC territory totals 14.73%** — India 7.56 + Pakistan 6.03 + Bangladesh 1.14 — against
Egypt 5.58, UK 5.20 and UAE 3.81. **South Asia alone outweighs any single Western market.**

⚠️ **Territory note, recorded deliberately.** CLAUDE.md puts **Saudi Arabia in EMEA**: *"Gulf markets
(UAE, Saudi) are out of territory — they sit with EMEA… a Gulf-HQ'd company is not [in scope]."*
Riyadh Air is Gulf-HQ'd, so by the standing rule this account belongs to EMEA. **Prateek instructed
"take this one as exception" and that instruction is the sole basis for it sitting in an APAC
pipeline.** Recorded here so the exception is never mistaken for a territory ruling.

### Two rows that need care before anyone builds an argument on them

**🇮🇳 India — biggest non-home market, weakest engagement.** 7.56% share but **2.27 pages/visit**, the
lowest of any top-9 market, on a 56.73% bounce and a ▼39.30% decline. Compare Saudi at 5.01 pages
and the UAE at 5.27. India is generating **look-to-book behaviour, not booking behaviour** — a lot of
searching, little depth. Treat Indian volume as demand interest, **not as transaction volume**, until
route-serving status is confirmed.

**🇧🇩 Bangladesh — the opposite, and the most interesting row here.** Only 1.14% share, but the
**longest sessions in the entire dataset at 06:36**, 3.95 pages, and a low **27.39%** bounce — against
Egypt's 01:56 at 64.20% bounce. Six-and-a-half-minute sessions are booking-flow behaviour. **Small,
growing (▲16.10%) and deeply engaged.** A real rail gap in Bangladesh would cost conversions that are
already most of the way to a purchase.

### Country rank is the sleeper metric

Riyadh Air ranks **#343 in Saudi Arabia** — a top-350 site nationally. But also **#2,871 in the UAE**
and **#3,192 in Pakistan**, both far stronger than their traffic share alone implies, and far ahead of
India's #34,127. **Pakistan is punching well above its 6.03% share**, and is the one market where
share and national rank agree that this is a serious audience.

### Caveats to carry into the report
- **The all-country-domains toggle is OFF.** If Riyadh Air runs any country-specific domain, that
  traffic is excluded and these shares are understated for the markets concerned. Not resolvable from
  the supplied view.
- **No total-visit figure was shown**, so no absolute visit count — and therefore **no transaction
  count** — can be derived from this file. Any volume figure must come from passenger or booking
  disclosures and be tagged accordingly.
- **Declines dominate the top 7.** India ▼39.30%, Egypt ▼40.22%, US ▼31.11%, Pakistan ▼24.33%, UAE
  ▼21.15% — against Saudi ▲34.24%. The business is **concentrating into its home market**, not
  diffusing. That cuts against a cross-border pitch and must be acknowledged, not hidden.
