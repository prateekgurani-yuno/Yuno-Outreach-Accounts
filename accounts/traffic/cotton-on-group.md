## What stands out before research

- **The US is the growth engine, not APAC.** 29.37% and ▲22.08%, with the **highest audience
  share of any country on the site (38.41%)** — higher than home-market Australia's 31.43%.
  That inversion (more of the audience than the traffic) says US visitors are a bigger share of
  unique people than of sessions.
- **South Africa at 12.58% is the #3 market** and growing 14.79%. That is **EMEA territory** —
  flag it to them; it is a material part of this merchant that is not Prateek's to pitch.
- **SEA is real but small in traffic terms: 3.83%.** Singapore ▲37.92% is the live signal.
  ⚠️ **Traffic understates this merchant's APAC footprint** — Cotton On runs physical stores
  across SEA, and in-store volume does not appear in SimilarWeb at all. Research must establish
  the store estate separately before scoring APAC presence off web traffic alone.
- **Private company** — no published transaction volume. The volume gate will likely be
  unverifiable, which per the rules cannot *reject* the account but also yields no sourced figure.
