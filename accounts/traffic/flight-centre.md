## 🛑 Read this before scoring

**This is a domestic ANZ account, not a pan-APAC one.** Australia plus New Zealand is 79.23% of
all traffic; everything in Asia combined is 2.25%. The standard APAC pitch — missing local rails
across Indonesia/India/Thailand — has almost no traffic behind it here. Per
`.claude/reference/apac-payments.md` and `/full-outreach` §6b, **ANZ is the one sub-region where
the routing, failover and cost-of-acceptance framing lands most directly**, so that is the angle,
not APMs.

**Two further caveats research must resolve:**

1. **Home market traffic is declining 10.73%** — the largest fall of any top-5 country here.
2. **FY26 TTV was A$25.7bn, but A$12.7bn of that is the corporate division** (FCM/Corporate
   Traveller), which is largely invoiced B2B settlement rather than web checkout. **Do not treat
   A$25.7bn as addressable online card volume.** Establishing the leisure-vs-corporate and
   online-vs-store split is the first job of the research run.
3. **Singapore ▲1,386.11%** is the one genuine APAC signal — but off 0.48%, so it is a question
   to ask, not a claim to make.
