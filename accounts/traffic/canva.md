## What stands out before research

- **Indonesia #2 globally at 10.79%.** QRIS and virtual-account are the dominant online rails
  there. A card/wallet-only subscription checkout in that market is the single biggest question
  on this account.
- **Four more APAC rails markets in the top 12** — India (UPI, UPI Autopay for recurring),
  Philippines (GCash), Thailand (PromptPay), Vietnam (MoMo/ZaloPay). All are recurring-billing
  markets where the local rail and the card rail behave very differently.
- **This is a subscription business** — read `.claude/reference/subscription-payments.md` before
  drafting, and check §4 (app-store trap) early. Canva is primarily web-billed, but that needs
  confirming, not assuming.
- **Engagement is extreme and APAC-led**: Thailand 12.24 pages/visit, Japan 10:44 duration and
  9.32 pages, Brazil 10.68. These are working sessions, not browsing.
- **Home market Australia is not in the top 12.** Like Cettire, the Australian HQ is a buying
  centre, not the market.
