## 🛑 Read the engagement columns before using any of this

**Rows 2 and 3 are almost certainly not human traffic.**

| | Russia | Japan |
|---|---|---|
| Visit duration | **3 seconds** | **1 second** |
| Pages per visit | **1.13** | **1.03** |
| Bounce rate | **92.98%** | **94.83%** |

A one-second visit to a single page that bounces 95% of the time is a **crawler, scraper or
bot signature**, not a prospective borrower. Compare the Philippines row: **2:23 duration, 2.94
pages, 48.73% bounce** — that is real behaviour. **Treat Russia (5.09%) and Japan (3.18%) as noise
and do NOT build a Japan or Russia line on them.** Korea (5s, 38% bounce) and Australia (12s) are
also thin.

⚠️ **Spain and Kazakhstan both read ▼100.00%**, which is a data artefact rather than a readable
trend — it normally means the prior period's figure is gone, not that traffic fell to zero.
**Georgia at ▲>5,000% on 0.72% share is the same kind of artefact.** None of these is usable.
