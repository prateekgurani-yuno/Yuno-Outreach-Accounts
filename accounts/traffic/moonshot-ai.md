## 🛑 READ THIS BEFORE USING ANY OF THE ABOVE

### The dataset describes the corporate site, not the product. Resolved by me by curl, 2026-09-30.

```
moonshot.ai          → www.moonshot.ai        200, corporate site — does NOT fold into kimi
kimi.moonshot.cn     → 301 → www.kimi.com     the China consumer product moved to kimi.com
platform.moonshot.ai → 301 → platform.kimi.ai international developer platform
platform.moonshot.cn → 301 → platform.kimi.com China developer platform
www.kimi.com         → 200                    THE PRODUCT
```

**`moonshot.ai` is a separate corporate/brand domain. It does not redirect to `kimi.com`, and
`kimi.com` does not appear in this dataset.** With the country-domain toggle OFF, the table
above therefore measures **the marketing site**, while the consumer product (`kimi.com`), the
international developer platform (`platform.kimi.ai`) and the China developer platform
(`platform.kimi.com`) are all excluded.

**The engagement metrics corroborate this independently.** Visit durations of 00:00:22 to
00:02:48, pages/visit of 1.49–2.30, and bounce rates up to 75.03% are brochure-site numbers,
not product numbers. Nobody *uses* a product for 44 seconds at 1.71 pages a visit.

> **Consequence:** the country profile below cannot be used to size the business, and the
> APM gap analysis built on it is describing where people read *about* Kimi, not where they
> pay for it. **Ask Prateek for `kimi.com` with the toggle ON before anything quantitative.**
> Every ICP signal that depends on the country split is marked ⬜ in the report for this reason.
