## ⚠️ Analyst notes — read before using this table

**1. There is no home market. Singapore does not appear in the top 11 at all.**
Manus is Singapore-HQ'd, yet the largest single market is **Brazil at 17.76%**, and no country
reaches 18%. The ICP row *"High traffic outside home (home country < 60%)"* therefore fires
**decisively** — this is one of the most internationally distributed accounts in the pipeline.

**2. Extreme fragmentation: 121 countries, top market under 18%, and 42% of traffic outside the
top 11.** This is the structural profile orchestration exists for. It is also the opposite of a
"pick two markets and localise" account — any rail strategy has to be portfolio-wide.

**3. ✅ The engagement is REAL, and unusually strong.** Unlike thin international traffic seen on
other accounts in this repo, every top market shows **6–8 minute sessions, 4–6 pages per visit
and 32–40% bounce**. **Vietnam is exceptional at 15:36 and 12.80 pages/visit.** These are
genuine working sessions, not bounce traffic. Treat all eleven as real markets.
The two weakest are **Thailand (2:25, 53.30% bounce)** and **Taiwan (48.83% bounce)**, which are
merely ordinary rather than suspect.

**4. 🚩 THE BIG CAVEAT — traffic is contracting almost everywhere.**
**Ten of the eleven top markets are DOWN**, several steeply: Vietnam ↓47.20%, UK ↓41.83%,
Japan ↓33.49%, Egypt ↓30.91%, Korea ↓30.84%, US ↓28.22%, India ↓26.62%, Brazil ↓21.22%,
Taiwan ↓20.75%, Thailand ↓20.02%. **Only Saudi Arabia is up (↑6.87%).**
This is a company-wide contraction over the quarter, consistent with a cooling hype cycle around
AI agents. **Consequences:**
- Do **not** write a "rapid international expansion" narrative off this table. The footprint is
  wide, but it is shrinking almost everywhere.
- Any transaction-volume estimate derived from traffic is a **declining** base, so prefer the
  most recent month over a quarterly average, and label the trend.
- Commercially this can *help* the pitch: a company whose traffic is falling is more interested
  in conversion and approval rates than one whose traffic is doubling. Frame carefully and never
  tell them their traffic is collapsing.

**5. Audience-share pattern is healthy, and differs by market.** India's audience share (7.39%)
**exceeds** its traffic share (5.65%) — many distinct users, fewer repeat visits each. Brazil is
the reverse (14.93% vs 17.76%) — fewer users, visiting more often. Neither pattern suggests
inflated traffic; both are consistent with a real product.
