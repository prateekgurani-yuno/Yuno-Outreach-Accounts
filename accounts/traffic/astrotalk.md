## What stands out before research

- **This is a diaspora business, not a multi-market one.** India is 77.30% and growing 15.58%; the next 17.52% is **US, UK, Canada, Australia, UAE, Singapore and New Zealand** — the classic Indian-diaspora corridor. Every one of those markets is an Indian consumer buying an Indian service from abroad.
- ⚠️ **India at 77.30% is above the 60% threshold**, so the ICP "high traffic outside home" signal (+2) **does not fire.** That is correct, not an oversight.
- **UAE has the best engagement on the site by a wide margin** — 6.94 pages/visit and 3:14, against India's 4.00 and 2:28, on a **#7,227 country rank**. The most engaged audience is in the Gulf. ⚠️ **Gulf is EMEA territory** — flag it, do not pitch it.
- **UK is collapsing: ▼34.62%.** Canada ▼17.65%, Australia ▼15.86%, Norway ▼64.51%, NZ ▼65.63%. The whole English-speaking diaspora is shrinking while India grows.
- **Singapore ▲988.08%** — but at a **0:08 average visit and 1.22 pages**, which reads like bot or redirect traffic rather than real users. Treat with suspicion; do not build a Singapore story on it.
- ⚠️ **Toggle was OFF**, so any country-specific Astrotalk domain is excluded from this view.
- ⚠️ **Web traffic may badly understate this business.** Astrotalk is app-first with a wallet-recharge model; app recharges do not appear in SimilarWeb at all. Establish the web-vs-app split before sizing anything from this table.
